# Design context
User-supplied brief: an Arabic-first premium law firm demo for a prospective client requesting custom interactive 3D. Visitors are Arabic-speaking individuals and businesses exploring practice areas and considering a consultation. Tone: authority, trust, precision, discreet luxury. Fictional brand رِواق. Charcoal, warm metallic gold, cream; architectural composition, editorial hierarchy, generous spacing. No fabricated claims, statistics, testimonials, or contact details. Local-only Vite/React implementation.
